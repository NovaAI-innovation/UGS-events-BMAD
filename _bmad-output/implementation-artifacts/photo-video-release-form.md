## Notes for Use

### When to Use This Form
- At event registration/check-in
- For performers and artists
- For VIP guests and special participants
- For anyone prominently featured in event media
- For minors (with parent/guardian signature)

### Best Practices
1. **Obtain signatures before or at the event**
2. **Keep forms organized by event**
3. **Store securely for record-keeping**
4. **Have forms available in multiple languages if needed**
5. **Provide clear explanation of what participants are signing**

### Alternative: General Event Release
For large events where individual releases are impractical, consider:
- Posted notices at event entrances
- Terms in ticket purchase agreement
- General release language in event terms

- Consult with legal counsel before use
- Ensure compliance with local privacy laws
- Consider GDPR requirements for international events
- Review state/provincial laws regarding image rights
