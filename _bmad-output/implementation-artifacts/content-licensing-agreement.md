## Notes for Use

1. **Customize for Each Agreement:** This is a template. Modify terms based on specific content and needs.

2. **Legal Review:** Have all licensing agreements reviewed by legal counsel before signing.

3. **Clear Specifications:** Be specific about what content is licensed, how it can be used, and for how long.

4. **Compensation Terms:** Clearly define payment terms, amounts, and schedules.

5. **Attribution:** Specify exactly how credit will be given.

6. **Term and Territory:** Clearly define the scope and duration of the license.
