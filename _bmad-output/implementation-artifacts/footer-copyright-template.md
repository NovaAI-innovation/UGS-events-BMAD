## Implementation Notes

- **Year:** Update annually or use dynamic year (e.g., `new Date().getFullYear()`)
- **Company Name:** Always use full name "Underground Sound Events"
- **Placement:** Include on every page, typically at bottom of viewport
- **Styling:** Ensure copyright text is visible but not intrusive
- **Mobile:** Consider condensed version for smaller screens

## Legal Requirements

- Copyright symbol (©) or word "Copyright"
- Year of first publication (2026)
- Name of copyright owner (Underground Sound Events)
- "All rights reserved" statement (recommended)
