## Notes for Use

1. **Customize for Each Engagement:** Modify terms based on specific artist, event, and compensation structure.

2. **Legal Review:** Have all contracts reviewed by legal counsel before signing.

3. **Clear Terms:** Be specific about performance details, compensation, and IP rights.

4. **Payment Terms:** Clearly define payment schedule and methods.

5. **IP Rights:** Carefully consider what recording/photography rights are needed.

6. **Cancellation:** Clearly define cancellation terms and force majeure provisions.
