## 2. Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe that content on the Underground Sound Events website infringes your copyright, you may submit a DMCA takedown notice. Your notice must include the following information:

### 2.1 Required Information

1. **Physical or Electronic Signature**
   - Signature of the copyright owner or authorized agent

2. **Identification of Copyrighted Work**
   - Description of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed
   - URL or location of the original work (if available)
   - Registration number (if applicable)

3. **Identification of Infringing Material**
   - Specific URL(s) where the infringing material is located
   - Clear description of the infringing content
   - Sufficient information to locate the material

4. **Contact Information**
   - Your name, address, telephone number, and email address
   - If acting as an agent, identification of the copyright owner

5. **Good Faith Statement**
   - Statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law

6. **Accuracy Statement**
   - Statement that the information in the notice is accurate
   - Statement, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner

Submit your DMCA takedown notice to:

## 4. Counter-Notification

### 4.1 Right to Counter-Notify
If you believe your content was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notification.

### 4.2 Counter-Notification Requirements

Your counter-notification must include:

1. **Physical or Electronic Signature**
   - Your signature

2. **Identification of Removed Material**
   - Description of the material that was removed
   - Location where it appeared before removal

3. **Good Faith Statement**
   - Statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification

4. **Consent to Jurisdiction**
   - Your name, address, and telephone number
   - Consent to the jurisdiction of federal court in [Jurisdiction]
   - Consent to accept service of process from the person who filed the original notice

5. **Contact Information**
   - Your full contact details

### 4.3 Counter-Notification Process
- Submit counter-notification to: [dmca@undergroundsoundevents.com]
- We will forward the counter-notification to the original complainant
- If the original complainant does not file a court action within 10-14 business days, we may restore the content
